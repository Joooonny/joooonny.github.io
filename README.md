## 🎮 Apps

### 🗣️ Don't say it!

**The party game where the word is on the tip of your tongue, but you can't say it.**

Describe the secret word to your team without using the forbidden words, before the timer runs out.

- **Classic** and **Versus** modes for teams or head-to-head play
- **Bonus rounds**: mime it, rhyme it, whistle it
- Many categories: food, cinema, music, animals, sport, science, and more
- Available in 🇮🇹 Italian, 🇬🇧 English, 🇫🇷 French, 🇪🇸 Spanish, 🇩🇪 German

[**Download on the App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6831318329) · [Privacy policy](https://joooonny.github.io/dontsayit-legal/privacy/)

### 🧱 Unwall

**A fresh take on match-3: you don't just rearrange tiles, you move through them.**

Guide a circle across a grid of pastel walls. Swap the colors of adjacent walls, line up three or more of the same color, and they fade away to open your path.

- **4 difficulty modes**, from 3 to 6 colors, each with a larger grid
- Collect coins on the grid for bonus moves or extra seconds
- Simple to learn, hard to put down

[**Download on the App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760947069)
